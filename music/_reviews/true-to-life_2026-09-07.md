## The short of it

A Tiny House does more with four minutes than most records do with forty. The guitars
feel recorded through a pillow, the vocals sit half-buried in the mix, and the hooks
appear when you least expect them.

## What it sounds like

- Dreamy, gauzy guitar pop with a shoegaze underbelly
- Hushed, conversational vocals that never shout
- Big choruses hiding under four tracks of reverb

> Listen with the volume up and the lights off.

## Standouts

The opener kicks the door in quietly, but the real gem is *Skyline*, which builds a
whole suburb of melody out of two chords. Great.
